# Component Context

- **Исток first-person sandbox** (`src/pages/Sandbox/`): Primary survival experience with streamed smooth heightfield terrain, desert/forest/snow biomes, selected-item-only first-person display, practice, individual physiology, solo saves and up to four SSE-connected players. Detailed components in `docs/agents/context/survival.md`; procedural character/tools/wildlife in `docs/agents/context/sandbox-art.md`. Previous driving expedition remains at `/expedition`.

- **BeaconLandmarks** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/BeaconLandmarks.tsx`): Five distinct terrain-following silhouettes around the beacon sites: solar stones, a living timber gate, a ridge watchtower, resonant columns and stepped sunset terraces. They provide visual navigation without a prescribed road and are composed by `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/TownScene.tsx`.
- **Expedition site clearance** (`src/lib/expedition.ts`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/world/generate.ts`): Procedural solid placement reserves wider landmark sites and individual crystal pickup areas while leaving travel routes open to player choice.
- **Expedition mission interaction** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`): Every beacon uses the same discover, collect three crystals and restore flow. Discovery notices identify the local landmark; restoring Dawn also announces the permanent drive reward.
- **Car reward presentation** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CarModel.tsx`): Reads saved expedition upgrades, applies tuned physics after Dawn restoration, publishes speed to mutable town state, and adds visible gold trim to the upgraded vehicle.
- **Driving HUD** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`): Adds a ref-driven km/h display and reward stats. Atlas target entries identify discovered landmarks; Escape controls include Space handbrake.
- **Dynamic follow camera** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CameraRig.tsx`): Speed expands follow distance, raises the camera, looks farther down the road and widens FOV while preserving the smoothed behind-car orbit.

- **Pause presentation** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CameraRig.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/InteractionTooltip.tsx`): Camera stops and hides the interaction tooltip during Escape pause; tooltip background follows the shared transparency setting.

- **Pause and camp placement** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/state.ts`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CarModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Crates.tsx`): Escape opens the pause menu and pauses movement, interaction and crates, then clears held input. Closing/unmounting resumes. Camp is offset from the player spawn to avoid overlapping its monument.

- **Camp rescue reset** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/state.ts`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CarModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`): Explicit mutable teleport revision resets velocity and rollover even when R returns the car from within five units of camp.

- **Portfolio integration** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`): Passes mutable town state to the expedition HUD; renders the car directly without character selection or switching handlers.

- **ExpeditionWorld** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`): Camp, five restoration monuments, energy crystals and light columns. Frame-based proximity discovery and height-aware pickups dispatch discrete Effector events; F restores/completes, R rescues to camp. Composed in `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/TownScene.tsx`.

## Page (`src/pages`)

- **PortfolioPage** (`src/pages/Portfolio`): Route `/` — a bright "diorama" top-down town (react-three-fiber). A voxel car moves freely in a room with multiple altar signs; approaching an altar highlights it (pulsing accent ring + light), shows a tooltip, pressing E triggers a transition (the camera slowly dollies to the altar name while the screen fades to white), then navigates to the altar's target route.
  - `lib/TownScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — sky/fog atmosphere, hemisphere + warm sun + cool rim lights, drei `SoftShadows`, `Terrain`, `Props`, `Pedestals`, the 3×3 crate stack, the car, follow camera. Renders `Pedestals` with the mutable town state, and the single `CarModel` with a fixed car collision radius. There is **no fence** — the open world is bounded by the generated 8×6 chunk area.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: World grid tokens (`CHUNK_SIZE` 16, `WORLD_COLS` 6, `WORLD_ROWS` 4, `TERRAIN_SEED`), player spawn position, interaction timing, camera tuning (base/near-pedestal FOV, blend speeds, interaction zoom, `CAMERA_FAR`), and atmosphere tokens (bright cartoon sky `#bfe3f5`, fog near/far, sun position). There is no map-boundary clamp.
  - `lib/Terrain.tsx`: Open-world ground — one height-mapped `PlaneGeometry` mesh per active chunk (always an 8×6 = 48-chunk window). Vertices are displaced by `world.terrain.heightAt`; per-vertex colors blend the biome `low`/`high` palette and a tileable deterministic `1024×1024` nearest-pixel detail texture adds 4× denser micro-grain without increasing terrain geometry. `flatShading` + gentle height quantization give the cartoon voxel look. A large distant plane fills the fogged horizon. Subscribes to streaming revisions; retains existing chunk geometry from `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/world/geometry.ts`, and only mounts newly prepared terrain meshes. Geometry lifetime belongs to the streaming cache; the detail texture/material are disposed on unmount.
  - `lib/Props.tsx`: Shared instanced geometry for broadleaf/columnar trees and tiered firs with roots, branching trunks and crown highlights; irregular faceted rocks with satellite stones and moss/mineral accents. Fixed-capacity GPU buffers survive streaming shifts; small grass/flower details receive but do not cast shadows. Biome tints color stones and grass. Instance buffers and shared component geometry are disposed on unmount; cleanup calls the prototype disposer because R3F sets the instance `dispose` prop to null.
  - `lib/Pedestals.tsx`: Stone arcade portal frames with emissive inlays, floating snake/letter emblems, compact dark billboard plaques, E badges and pulsing activation rings, reading the mutable hovered id each frame. One permanently mounted point light follows the active pedestal; the light count stays constant when chunks enter/leave, avoiding scene-wide shader recompilation stalls.
  - `lib/WorldStreamer.tsx`: Per-frame bridge from the mutable player position to `updateWorldStreaming(x, z)`. When the player enters a new chunk, the deterministic cache generates missing chunks and publishes a new active 8×6 window; old chunks remain cached but are no longer rendered or collidable.
  - `lib/Crates.tsx`: Streamed interactive supply piles rendered in one fixed-capacity instanced mesh. Mutable block state survives nearby chunk shifts via a weak cache keyed by spawn props; evicted regions respawn deterministically. Sleeping piles beyond 18 units skip physics; moving crates continue settling. Player contact uses actual mutable player height, including flight; teleports suppress player/crate contacts. `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/cratePhysics.ts` handles approach-only impacts, stack transfer, bounce and three-axis tumbling. Instance buffers use the prototype disposer on unmount (compatible with R3F `dispose={null}`); crate geometry is also disposed. Shared procedural geometry has inset wood, framing, braces and nails.
  - `lib/CarModel.tsx`: Voxel car with steering/spinning wheels, driven by `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/carPhysics.ts`. Publishes physical player height for crate collisions; follows spring suspension height and inertial pitch/roll; smoothly aligns to slopes while supported and retains its ground orientation in flight. Slope rollover is gated by ground contact and disabled on purpose-built ramps; movement uses the combined terrain/ramp surface. Teleports reset velocity, chassis attitude and contact state. Uses shared obstacle collision and interaction helpers.
  - `lib/CameraRig.tsx`: Third-person follow camera orbits behind the shared player facing for the car, including portal interactions. Shortest-angle exponential smoothing preserves orbit distance through U-turns; per-instance refs avoid stale poses after remount. Tracks physical player height in flight, freezes on Escape, retains pedestal highlighting/tooltips and contextual FOV.
  - `lib/AutoloopBridge.tsx`: Test-only bridge mounted inside the Canvas by `PortfolioPage`. When `?autoloop=1` is present it exposes `window.__autoloop` (`freeze`, `hideHud`, `setSeed`, `setPlayer`, `stats`) and measures FPS. It also sets `gl.info.autoReset = false` and resets the renderer info each frame, so `stats()` reports the whole-frame draw calls/triangles (postprocessing included) instead of only the last pass. Runtime commands validate numeric input and clean up freeze/HUD state on unmount. `freeze(true)` sets a shared flag (`src/lib/autoloop.ts`) that makes `CarModel`, `Crates`, and `CameraRig` skip their `useFrame` updates, producing a static frame for screenshot capture. `hideHud` toggles the `autoloop-hide-hud` class on `<html>`, which hides elements marked with `data-hud` (see `HubHud`).
  - `lib/state.ts`: Mutable game state ref (car position/facing, interaction timer, `interactionTarget`, `hoveredPedestalId`, tooltip). Player includes physical `y` for crate contacts, written by `CarModel.tsx`. Initial `facing` is `Math.PI` so the character starts looking **away** from the follow camera.
  - `lib/controls.ts`: Keyboard input (WASD/arrows for movement/steering, E for interaction).
  - `ui/InteractionTooltip.tsx`: DOM overlay above the hovered pedestal — ref-driven via rAF (no React re-renders).
  - `ui/HubHud.tsx`: Expedition journal, energy and restoration counters, compass and contextual action hints; a circular always-visible mini-map keeps the player marker centered while its world layer moves and rotates with the camera heading. It shows beacons and remaining crystals and automatically highlights the next unrestored beacon. Uses Effector for discrete progress and mutable refs for continuous position. Carries `data-hud` for the autoloop harness.
  - `ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`: Canvas (fog/sky handled in the scene) + `EffectComposer` (soft `Bloom` + `Vignette`) + HubHud + tooltip + fade overlay + navigation callback. The fade overlay **slowly brightens to white** during activation (starts at ~25% of the animation, fully white by ~95%) then the route navigates.
- **LettersPage** (`src/pages/Letters`): Route `/letters` — Letter Rain. A full-screen 3D canvas; typing any printable character **anywhere on the page** spawns a single 3D letter above the top boundary. It falls and collides with the side/bottom bounds and with other letters (top stays open). The field is one row deep (`AREA_HALF_D` = 1.5 × letter depth) — letters are locked at z = 0 and pile into a single row. Letters never rotate around Y — they only tilt, and the tilt is **free and dynamic**: letters keep their landed angle, wobble on impact (no restore to a fixed flat pose). Each letter is at least 120×120 px on screen (recomputed from viewport/camera on resize). Cap: `MAX_LETTERS` (9) — the oldest letter is evicted when full.
  - `ui/LettersPage.tsx`: Full-screen layout. Mutable world in a ref; subscribes `letterTyped`/`clearLetters` Effector events and bumps a React counter so the letter list re-renders only on spawn/evict. A single window `keydown` handler (no input field): printable chars → `letterTyped`, Escape → back to the town. A fixed "← Назад" pill button (top-left) navigates back to `/`.
  - `ui/LettersScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — background color, ambient/directional/point lights, floor plane, translucent bounds walls (front/back at ± letter depth), `Letters`, the physics game loop, the camera rig, and Bloom. Camera fits the play box to the viewport each frame and writes `pxPerWorld`/`letterScale` into the world.
  - `ui/Letters.tsx`: One `<mesh>` per letter (drei-style `TextGeometry` from the shared font cache), memoized and keyed by id — old letters are never re-mounted/re-created when new ones spawn, only their transforms are synced from the world each frame. Uses a shared `MeshStandardMaterial` with a soft emissive for Bloom.
- **SnakePage** (`src/pages/Snake`): Route `/snake` — the 3D Snake game. Composes the 3D `<Canvas>` (Arena, Snake, Food, Shards, Effects, CameraRig, lights, Bloom) with DOM overlays (Hud, Screens).
  - `ui/GameLoop.tsx`: Orchestrates the game loop inside Canvas — resets the world when the store's `gameId` changes (new game only, not on resume), steps physics, dispatches score/HP/boost events.
  - `ui/PointerTracker.tsx`: Raycasts the mouse pointer onto the arena floor plane and writes the target point into `inputRef` for steering. Mouse steering is active **only while the left mouse button is held** and is **inverted** (the snake steers away from the cursor); releasing LMB stops steering (WASD keeps working).

## Feature Components (`src/components`)

- **Arena** (`src/components/Arena`): Renders the 3D arena — neon-grid floor (drei `Grid`) and 4 semi-transparent walls with glowing edges.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Wall thickness.
- **Snake** (`src/components/Snake`): Renders the snake as an `InstancedMesh` pool (head + chain), synced from world state each frame. Per-segment colors (head→tail gradient) and orientation.
  - `lib/render.ts`: Per-frame matrix/color sync for the instanced chain. Unused instances (beyond the current segment count) are scaled to 0 every frame to keep them invisible.
- **Food** (`src/components/Food`): Renders "protected" food — translucent shell sphere with a glowing core. Pool of `MAX_FOODS` slots synced from world state.
- **Shards** (`src/components/Shards`): Renders shard pickups as a single `InstancedMesh` octahedron pool (1 draw call). Per-instance colors distinguish edible (amber, from theme) from inert just-broken (gray).
- **CameraRig** (`src/components/CameraRig`): Follow camera behind the head with smoothing, FOV kick on boost, orbit mode on the menu screen.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Camera offsets, FOV values, orbit params.
- **Effects** (`src/components/Effects`): Ambient dust points + a particle burst pool triggered on shell breaks.
  - `lib/particles.ts`: CPU particle simulation (positions/colors/lifetimes, circle sprite texture).
  - `lib/constants.ts`: Particle counts, speed, lifetime.
- **Hud** (`src/components/Hud`): DOM overlay — score, best score, HP hearts, boost bar. The boost bar is ref-driven (updated in a `requestAnimationFrame` loop straight from the mutable world, no React re-renders) and exposed as `role="progressbar"`; hearts carry an `aria-label`. The boost bar dims and shows a `КД X.Xс` countdown while the boost cooldown is active.
  - `lib/styles.ts`: styled-components for the HUD.
- **Screens** (`src/components/Screens`): DOM overlays for `menu` / `paused` / `gameover` phases — title, rules, controls, play buttons, social links (react-social-icons). Handles Esc-pause and Space/Enter-start hotkeys. Dialogs use `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal` and a Tab focus trap (`useFocusTrap`). Each panel has a "Назад" button that resets to the menu (`toMenu`) and navigates back to `/`.
  - `lib/styles.ts`: styled-components for screens/panels/buttons.

- **Ramps** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Ramps.tsx`): Three reusable instanced ramp meshes with curved decks, yellow/cream edge markings, direction arrows and wooden supports. Deck and edge-marking heights follow the analytic ramp profile; buffers survive chunk shifts and use the prototype disposer on unmount, compatible with R3F `dispose={null}`.

- **Expedition HUD** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`): Replaces portfolio branding with expedition journal, energy, progress, notices, context action hints and compass distance. A small circular top-right mini-map continuously shows nearby crystals and beacons around a fixed central player dot. Its ref-driven world transform follows position and the smoothed camera heading; the next unrestored beacon becomes the navigation target automatically.


- **HUD and Escape menu** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`): Atlas stays live and has no close icon; circular survey map and compact destination rows. Escape opens a focus-trapped pause dialog with controls (portal hint removed), resume button and persistent transparency slider. Continuous navigation/prompt text remains ref-driven; only Escape controls mutable pause. Bottom overlay contains contextual interaction only.

- **Paused world ornaments** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Pedestals.tsx`): Portal emblem and light animation clocks advance only during gameplay, so Escape freezes their pose and resumes without a time jump.

- **Car-only scene** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/TownScene.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/state.ts`): CarModel is the sole player entity; no character selector, toggle event, selection prop/type, walking animation state or switching hint remains.

## Current Sandbox replacement

- **SkyAtmosphere** (`src/pages/Sandbox/lib/SkyAtmosphere.tsx`): Camera-centered gradient sky dome with a warm sun disk and raymarched cloud volumes. Cloud count and sampling steps follow the graphics quality setting; `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx` aligns the sunlight and distant fog with the sky.

`src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/FirstPerson.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/terrain.ts`, and `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxHud.tsx` implement smooth-terrain survival with near/medium/far infinite chunk streaming, original birch/oak and four new Blender tree GLB shapes, four smoothly blended desert/forest/snow ground materials, surface-following movement with per-tree trunk collision, ground-seated props, biome-colored ground cover and volumetric mist, selected-item-only first-person display, individual physiology, two physical slots and no goals/hints. `WorldDeer` in `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx` renders animals separately from resource items and interpolates each animal's position and turn toward its persisted travel heading using mutable refs. Apple-bearing oaks carry harvestable fruit. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/RockModel.tsx` and `DeerModel.tsx` use textured GLBs for stones and rigged wildlife; `AppleModel.tsx` renders picked apples. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/materials.ts` and `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/textures/` hold the surface, tree and prop texture sources. Prior Quiet Shore component descriptions are historical. See `docs/agents/context/survival.md` and `docs/agents/context/sandbox-art.md`.
