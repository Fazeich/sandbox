# Component Context

- **Исток first-person sandbox** (`src/pages/Sandbox/`): Primary survival experience with streamed smooth heightfield terrain, desert/forest/snow biomes, selected-item-only first-person display, practice, individual physiology, solo saves and up to four SSE-connected players. Detailed components in `docs/agents/context/survival.md`; procedural character/tools/wildlife in `docs/agents/context/sandbox-art.md`. Previous driving expedition remains at `/expedition`.

## Sandbox components

- **SkyAtmosphere** (`src/pages/Sandbox/lib/SkyAtmosphere.tsx`): Camera-centered gradient sky dome with a warm sun disk and raymarched cloud volumes. Cloud count and sampling steps follow the graphics quality setting; `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx` aligns the sunlight and distant fog with the sky.

`src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/FirstPerson.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/terrain.ts`, and `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxHud.tsx` implement smooth-terrain survival with near/medium/far infinite chunk streaming, original birch/oak and four new Blender tree GLB shapes, four smoothly blended desert/forest/snow ground materials, surface-following movement with per-tree trunk collision, ground-seated props, biome-colored ground cover and volumetric mist, selected-item-only first-person display, individual physiology, two physical slots and no goals/hints. `WorldDeer` in `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx` renders animals separately from resource items and interpolates each animal's position and turn toward its persisted travel heading using mutable refs. Apple-bearing oaks carry harvestable fruit. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/RockModel.tsx` and `DeerModel.tsx` use textured GLBs for stones and rigged wildlife; `AppleModel.tsx` renders picked apples. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/materials.ts` and `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/textures/` hold the surface, tree and prop texture sources. Prior Quiet Shore component descriptions are historical. See `docs/agents/context/survival.md` and `docs/agents/context/sandbox-art.md`.
