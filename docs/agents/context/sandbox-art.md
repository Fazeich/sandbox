## Current primitive survival art

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/SkyAtmosphere.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`: Camera-centered blue gradient sky dome adds a warm sun disk and horizon haze aligned with the directional light. Twelve distributed cloud volumes raymarch animated 3D density through multiple irregular lobes inside shallow boxes, with shaded undersides; low quality renders six volumes with fewer steps. The clouds remain above the world as the camera moves, while terrain uses matching blue-green distance fog.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/PrimitiveArt.tsx`: `PrimitiveHands` now renders only the selected item as a camera-local view model; an empty selection hides the display, and no hands or offhand item are rendered. Held long/bulky objects use reduced scales and the selected item swings on action. `PrimitivePerson` accepts optional `bag`/`backpack` booleans to display crafted leather storage and simple worn straps; both default to false.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/VolumetricFog.tsx`: deterministic world-anchored mist puffs use a multi-octave animated shader, warm desert / mint forest / cool snow tints, distance fading and quality-scaled density. They follow streamed chunks and layer over the scene's linear fog without additional lights.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/PrimitiveArt.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/DeerModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/RockModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/textures/skin.png`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/materials.ts`: `PrimitivePerson({gender,moving})` renders block-shaped adult male/female avatars with textured skin, bare feet and modest shorts; female avatars also wear a sports bra. No clothing, backpack or tools are automatically equipped. `PrimitiveHands({item,active})` shows only the selected item, with no bare hands when the slot is empty. `WildAnimal({moving,dead})` clones the rigged deer GLB, uses the shared `src/lib/assetLighting.ts` outdoor fill, plays its authored relaxed walk from a mutable motion ref, and settles dead deer into a lying pose. `ItemVisual({kind,scale})` uses the low-poly GLBs for stone and apple, plus food, textured stone/wood tools, hides and primitive bags/backpacks. Other items use cached merged colour meshes. No lights, text or goals are included.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/TreeInstances.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/models/forest_trees.glb`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/models/apple.glb`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/textures/trees/`: `ForestInstances({trees})` draws original birch/oak plus spreading oak, forked birch, fan rowan and windswept oak from `Portfolio-models.blend` in instanced meshes with the shared `src/lib/assetLighting.ts` material fill. ## Previous expedition art (retained, no longer the active concept)

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/ExpeditionArt.tsx`: bundled procedural low-poly expedition art in cream, teal and ochre. `Explorer` is an animated 1.75m avatar facing local +Z with goggles, headlamp and bedroll backpack; limb movement uses refs, and its `moving` prop accepts either a boolean or mutable `{current:boolean}` read each frame to animate remote movement without React rerenders. `Hands` is camera-local first-person gloved hands with gathering pick or building hammer and plan, and ref-based swing animation. `Camp` is a shelter, bedroll, workbench with map/tool/first-aid board, warm lanterns, campfire and log seat. `BuildPiece` provides 2×2 floor (top y=.16), 2.4m window wall along X, lantern post and luminous beacon; transparent valid/invalid ghosts share the same silhouettes. Buildable lamps/beacons use emissive materials only; their bounded point-light budget belongs to the parent Island scene. All geometry/materials are code-native, no external assets or dependencies. Camp point lights do not cast additional shadow maps.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/index.ts`: named exports `Explorer`, `Hands`, `Camp`, `BuildPiece`, and the `BuildPieceKind` type for Sandbox-only use. Explorer origin is feet y=0; Hands attaches at camera origin and internally positions its tools around x±.3, y−.4, z−.6; static art anchors to ground y=0.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/SignalResponse.tsx`: `SignalResponse({position:[x,y,z]})` adds a persistent ambient response around the first active beacon: 28 small cream/amber motes spiral slowly upward in one Points draw call, and two procedural gulls circle 8–9m above it. Buffer/ref updates handle every animation without React state, dynamic lights or timers. Motes and birds explicitly disable raycasting so they cannot interfere with gathering/build picking. Exported from `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/index.ts`.
