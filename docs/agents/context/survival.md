# Current design: primitive first-person survival sandbox

Roadmap and assessment: `docs/ROADMAP.md` (work through it item by item).
Full spoiler map of every discoverable mechanic (never shown in game): `docs/MECHANICS.md`.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/FirstPerson.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/runtime.ts`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`: Main-menu camera is an endless slow dolly left → right (`MENU_DRIFT_SPEED` 1.15 m/s) that eases over terrain and rises above nearby canopies; it restarts near the player each time the menu opens. `runtime.view` is the point the world streams around (menu camera focus, or the player's feet in play); `syncStream` takes an optional center, and Island's sea, sun, fires and drops follow `view`.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/styles.css`: Cinematic main menu — letterbox bars and dawn fade on open, film grain, left scrim, staggered serif title «Исток», italic tagline, numbered serif actions (indices are `aria-hidden`, so button names stay «Продолжить», «Новая жизнь», «Вместе», «Настройки»), world code and «Ты всё ещё там.» / «Там осталось твоё тело.» for a save, «Мир просыпается…» until ready. Menu sheets (Вместе, Настройки, Новая жизнь) are a glass panel sliding from the right while the menu dims; the Esc pause is a left panel over the blurred world with the same type system (kicker «Мир замер» / «Мир продолжает жить», pill «Продолжить», numbered items, world code). Inventory is a centred glass card. Leaving the menu plays a short fade from black (`origin-curtain`). Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (display) + Manrope (UI); tokens are CSS variables on `.shore`; reduced-motion disables animation.

- `src/lib/survival/items.ts`: Data table of all ~43 item kinds (`ITEM_KINDS`, `ITEMS`): accessibility name, food effects (hunger/thirst/stamina/health, sickness and its chance), tool powers (`hit`, `chop`, `cut`, `dig`, `probe`, `fish`, `hammer`), per-use `wear`, fire `fuel`, `cooksTo`, `spoils` seconds. `ITEM_NAMES`, `isItemKind`, `toolOf`.
- `src/lib/survival/recipes.ts`: Ordered `COMBINE_RULES` for two-handed work (cobble knapping with flake byproduct, flint → hand axe by practice, pounding nuts/clams/bones, bone awl, hand-drill fire needing dry grass dropped nearby, whittling, hafting a stone spear with cord, cord twisting, bag/backpack sewing sped by an awl) and `soloWork` (branch → stick + twig). `Ctx` gives rules clone-safe helpers (`roll`, `give`, `wear`, `tire`).
- `src/lib/survival/world.ts`: Second, independent forage pass per chunk (`addForage`, ids from `EXTRA_NODE_INDEX` = 100 so original objects never move): flint, ground branches, dry grass, berry bushes (blue/red), mushrooms (edible/toxic), termite mounds, rotten logs, nests (1–3 eggs), bones, shoreline clams, tuber plants. `NODE_TARGET_HEIGHT`, `NODE_CAPACITY`, `TREE_TWIGS`.
- `src/lib/survival/rules.ts`: Table-driven engine. `WorldState.fires` (seconds of fuel), `Person.sickness` (hidden 0–100), practice keys `knapping/cutting/weaving/firecraft/foraging/fishing`. Deterministic per-action rolls (`rollFor`: seed + revision + actor). `use` handles trees (chop, apples, bare-hand twigs), wildlife (hit sums, kick-back damage, butchering), bushes, termite probing, rotten-log grubs, tuber digging; `use` on `fire-*` feeds, cooks (freshness kept), hardens spears or burns a bare hand. `drink` with a fishing tool spears fish. `spoilItem`/`stepMatter` age perishable food in hands and on the ground into `rotten`; `stepFires` burns fuel; `nearFire` doubles healing and speeds sleep. `parseWorld` loads older saves without fires/sickness/new skills.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/WorldFeatures.tsx`: Flat-shaded placeholder forms for forage sources (`FeatureVisual`, shows remaining berries/eggs/clams) and `FireVisual` (stones, flame, point light scaled by fuel). `PrimitiveArt.tsx` has geometry for every item kind; `SandboxHud.tsx` has an icon for every kind and a sickness vignette (`origin-sick`). `Island.tsx` renders forage and fires; `FirstPerson.tsx` targets fires and uses per-kind aim heights. Server and solo tick `stepMatter` + `settleDeaths` each second.

- `src/lib/survival/rules.ts`: Hardcore death. `settleDeaths(state)` empties a dead person's slots into shared `body-*` drops around the body (bag/backpack are lost with it) and clears sleep; `applyAction` runs it after every accepted action, the room server after each tick, `tickSolo` after vitals (and saves immediately). `respawn` action (dead only) replaces the actor with `freshPerson` near the seed spawn: same world, empty hands, practice reset. `isDead` helper.
- `src/lib/survival/rules.ts`, `configs/coopServer.ts`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/session.ts`: `poseAllowed(seed, previous, next, elapsed)` — 12 m/s travel budget capped at 2 s, eye height within −1.5/+3 m of `terrainHeight`. Rejected poses reply `{ ok: false, pose }`; the client snaps back (`snapTo`) instead of marking the session lost, and a successful heartbeat restores `connected`. Respawn replies include the new pose and move the server-side peer.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/FirstPerson.tsx`: Death screen «Тишина.» offers «Родиться заново» (respawn) and «В меню»; pointer lock is released on death and blur/unlock no longer opens the pause dialog for the dead.

- `tests/e2e/survival.e2e.ts`: Built-browser checks for empty start, opt-in minimap, persistent render-distance selection, shader/WebGL errors, continuing/new random life, real mouse stone knapping, 21-cell backpack transfer with physical B, two independent browser clients receiving live join/leave events, and live room authority (shared seed, separate inventories, contested pickup and departure drops). `README.md` documents the current prototype scope and server requirements.

- `configs/coopServer.ts`: Departed/timed-out actors are removed; held items drop at their last position, preserving shared materials without accumulating inactive survivors.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/FirstPerson.tsx`: The camera-local display shows only the selected inventory item and disappears when its slot is empty; the opposite hand and bare hands are hidden. Physiology uses elapsed time independently of capped physics steps. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`: Other players wear bags/backpacks only after crafting. `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxPage.tsx`: Visiting the main menu alone does not create a save.

- `src/lib/survival/survival.test.ts`: Deterministic seed variation, coverage of all eight tree types with the smaller rowan more frequent than the tallest forked birch, wildlife heading in both flee directions and save restoration, dry spawns, unbounded travel, live 5×5/7×7/9×9 render-range changes and bounded cache, empty two-hand start, repeated primitive practice, exact 2→9→30 inventory capacities, contested pickup/proximity checks, item conservation, no passive stamina recovery, slow running expenditure, sleep/food recovery and delayed exhaustion damage, save validation.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxPage.tsx`, `src/stores/sandbox/sandbox.ts`: Game-ready signal is emitted after the first world frame so the menu cannot start before input handlers mount; pagehide preserves the latest solo survival snapshot. Error fallback handles unavailable WebGL.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/ui/SandboxHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/styles.css`: Hint-free HUD: only 2/9 item cells and the specified physiology dial (filled red health disk, lower orange satiety arc, upper blue hydration arc, outer yellow long-term stamina ring). Optional terrain minimap defaults off. No crosshair, target label, key legend, objective, recipes or action notifications. Separate 21-cell backpack menu plus nine quick cells, explicit sleep/wake, save replacement confirmation, settings (including persistent near/medium/far render distance) and shared-room setup outside gameplay. Current title: Исток.

- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/TreeInstances.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/models/forest_trees.glb`, `src/lib/survival/world.ts`: The streamed world instantiates original birch/oak, wide and fruiting oak forms, plus spreading oak, forked birch, fan rowan and windswept oak from `Portfolio-models.blend`. Spawn weights follow inverse squared modeled size, making smaller trees more common. Fruit instances remain on apple oaks and disappear as apples are gathered. `WorldDeer` in `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx` smoothly follows each wildlife target and turns toward its heading; trees, ground cover and wildlife sit on the same continuous surface.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/terrain.ts`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/materials.ts`, `src/lib/survival/world.ts`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/VolumetricFog.tsx`: Streamed terrain uses continuous smooth-shaded heightfields and smoothly interpolated grass/earth/sand/snow weights across every biome boundary. The same four bright surface maps cover forest, desert and snow regions. Instanced ground cover and warm/cool mist tints remain. The far preset stays within the 81-chunk cache. Loose objects, live/dead wildlife, dropped items and minimally clothed remote adults use the art package.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/FirstPerson.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/terrain.ts`: First-person movement follows the continuous rendered triangle surface with normal gravity and jumping; there are no voxel step checks or block faces. Grounding and sleeping camera height use the same interpolated surface as the terrain mesh. Swimming still uses the smooth world water height. Movement, survival actions and streaming are unchanged; only the selected item appears in first person.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/Island.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/PrimitiveArt.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/RockModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/AppleModel.tsx`: Loose resources, harvested apples and drops rest on the smooth surface; boulders are embedded slightly using their GLB bounds. Trees, cover and wildlife remain anchored to matching terrain heights.
- `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/DeerModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/art/models/deer.glb`: `WildAnimal` uses the updated rigged red deer from `Portfolio-models.blend`, plays its authored walk, and rests carcasses on their sides.

- `src/lib/survival/rules.ts`, `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/session.ts`, `configs/coopServer.ts`: Nearby living wildlife slowly flees players on land and returns toward its home region; movement is part of shared authority state, and corpses stay where killed. Wildlife stepping only visits chunks near active players.

- `configs/coopServer.ts`: Room seed is random once and shared by all peers. Each member has its own empty-hand survivor, gender, practice and physiology; resource removals/dropped items are shared and server-authoritative. Server ticks long-term physiology once per second, including sleep, and broadcasts snapshots. Coordinates have no island-radius constraint.

- `src/stores/sandbox/sandbox.ts`: Survival-only Effector state, random new life, version-2 solo save, settings (minimap off, medium render distance by default), individual actor identity. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/runtime.ts`: Mutable camera/peers and bounded, settings-driven terrain streaming. `src/pages/Sandbox/lib/session.ts`: Shared-world SSE rooms hold separate survival actors/inventories; room joins preserve the local render-distance choice while synchronizing the streamed window.

User-directed replacement of the temporary Quiet Shore objective/building demo. No objective, beacon, starter gear, camp, tutorial, targeting prompt, recipe list, unlock toast or completion screen. New life/new room selects a fresh random seed; continuing a save retains seed and state.

- `src/lib/survival/world.ts`: Seeded smooth infinite terrain with lakes and three biomes, deterministic per-chunk natural resources, dry random-seed spawn selection. Sliding 5×5/7×7/9×9 active 20m chunk windows with a bounded 81-chunk cache, without world-edge constraints.
- `src/lib/survival/rules.ts`, `src/lib/survival/world.ts`: Authoritative individual survival actors, health/hunger/thirst/stamina, two literal item slots initially. Hands-on stone/nut work, repeated stone knapping, cutting wood, twisting fiber, apple picking/eating, hunting and skinning. Berry resources are removed; older saved berries migrate to apples. Practice changes through actions; no skill/recipe UI. Hide/cord bag expands quick slots to nine; framed hide backpack adds 21 separately opened slots (30 total). Discrete immutable actions, proximity checks, durability, returned drops and version-2 save validation.

The HUD must honor the user's no-hints rule. Minimap starts off. B/physical KeyB (Russian И) opens the backpack only when acquired. Numbers choose existing quick slots. No synthetic inventory supplies or fixed handcrafted starter clearing.

- Current fatigue model in src/lib/survival/rules.ts: walking/standing never deplete or passively restore stamina. Running and manual actions slowly deplete it; food and sleep restore it. Zero stamina accumulates an exhaustion timer and eventually health loss. Sleep is an explicit state. HUD uses red health disk, orange lower satiety arc, blue upper hydration arc, outer yellow long-term stamina ring.
